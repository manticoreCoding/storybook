# KAN-5: Add privacy policy + terms of service

## Intent
Provide a simple page displaying links to the current terms of use and privacy policy for users of the service.

## Acceptance criteria
- A new page is accessible in the app containing links to the terms of use and privacy policy.

## Out of scope
- Collecting user agreement or consent checkboxes.
- Editing or rewriting the text of the terms of use and privacy policy documents.
